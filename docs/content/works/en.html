WHITEOUT

Team Members:

  • ChocolaMint: Game Design, Programming
  • UguisuAnn: 2D Art
  • Kaburagi.M: Music, Sound Effects

Submission to unity1week Game Jam お題「しろ」.

A game where you set on a journey to paint a black world white.

You have to paint the floor white to defeat enemies and progress, but you also can't walk past white areas without dying.

This is my first time attempting a 3D action game in a game jam, with Godot no less. No doubt one of the most ambitious projects I've ever participated in.

As the vast majority of participants began to use coding agents at full force, our game was one of the very few entries with zero trace of generative AI in its production.

Triangle Attack of the Three Fairies

Team Members:

  • ChocolaMint: Game Design, Programming
  • OpA: Art
  • ramaseta: Music

Submission to Touhou Game Jam 2026.

A strange little game where you form acute triangles to attack and obtuse triangles to defend. Controlling the three fairies at the same time proved to be quite the challenge to most players.

This game got featured on the Touhou Station livestream and was played by ZUN.

GEOCENTER

Team Members:

  • ChocolaMint: Game Design, Programming, UI Design
  • AMAASAGI: Art
  • 朱音あおも: Music

Submission to unity1week Game Jam お題「ひく」.

A game where the Earth tries to make geocentrism a reality by defeating every single celestial body in the solar system in a grand tournament of tug-of-war.

The game was originally submitted to unityroom, with the itch.io reupload having cleaned up graphics instead.

KANAKO vs. SUIKA

Team Members:

  • ChocolaMint: Game Design, Programming, Ingame Sprites
  • BUNPPANG: Background, CG Illustrations
  • ramaseta: Music, Sound Effects

Submission to Touhou Fan Game Jam 15.

A Touhou-themed spin on Yoshi's Island's final boss. Play as Kanako and fend off the giant oni Suika's advance!

A very rare case where I did both the programming and some of the pixel art.

Hakase VS Kaiju

Team Members:

  • ChocolaMint: Game Design, Programming
  • Rioku: Art
  • xiel: Music, Sound Effects

Submission to unity1week Game Jam お題「1ボタン」.

A simple game where you play as a little inventor "Hakase-chan", who has to keep coming up new weapons to keep the Kaiju from taking over her toy island.

The catch is that all of her weapons involve the use of a single button, matching the jam theme.

The game placed 8th overall, and managed to get 1st in the Fun category, out of nearly 400 entries. (Results at the time of ranking announcements)

Amebrella

Team Members:

  • ChocolaMint: Game Design, Programming
  • ホロウサ: Art
  • なんかすいすい: Music, Sound Effects

Submission to unity1week Game Jam お題「ふる」.

An action platformer where you play as a robot girl with an umbrella, fighting against a gigantic titan that controls the weather.

The game featured textless tutorials and a very cinematic boss fight.

It placed 14th on average out of nearly 400 entries, and was a very fun experience for me overall.
